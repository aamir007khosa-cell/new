Features

Everything you need on site, from survey to alignment.

🧭

Direction & distance

Enter ISP and Client tower coordinates — get exact bearing, distance (km/m) and a straight line on the map.

📱

Live compass

Phone-style dial: point the front of your phone and the app tells you how many degrees to turn.

🗼

Tower height design

Terrain, trees, Fresnel zone and earth curvature — see the minimum ISP and Client tower height.

📶

Expected signal

Estimated dBm for Mimosa C6x with original dish and C6x Lite with a 30 dBi dish.

📡

Frequency planner

Mimosa-style center frequency and 20/40/80/160 MHz bandwidth suggestions.

⚡

Live Mimosa data

Connect multiple radios: live RSSI with beep, SNR, MCS, streams, speed and full link stats.

🛰️

Satellite map

Clear satellite map, fullscreen view, tap on map to set a tower, offline area download.

🔄

Auto updates

New features arrive automatically — no need to reinstall the app.

Customer support

Quick answers first. Still stuck? Open a ticket — we reply on Telegram or email.

Where do I find my Device Code?

Open the app. If you have no license, the lock screen shows it. If you already have one, open the License sheet. Tap “Copy code”.

How long until I get my key?

After the USDT transaction is confirmed on the blockchain and the proof is received — usually minutes, at most a few hours.

My key says “wrong device”.

Each key works only on the phone whose Device Code was sent. Check the code you sent matches the one in the app, then contact support.

I changed / reset my phone.

A factory reset or new phone gives a new Device Code. Contact support with the old and new code.

I sent on the wrong network.

Contact support immediately with the TXID. Funds sent on the wrong network or as the wrong coin may not be recoverable.

The app is not installing.

Uninstall any older copy first, allow “Install unknown apps”, and make sure you downloaded the full file. Android 6.0 or newer is required.

Mimosa radio is not connecting.

Your phone must be on the same network as the radio. Check the IP, username and password in the Radios tab. HTTPS is supported.
