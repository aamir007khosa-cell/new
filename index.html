    📡 Expected signal

    Coordinates se andaza: link par kitna signal (dBm) aayega aur kaun si bandwidth chalegi.

    China wali dish asal mein aksar likhe se 1–3 dB kam deti hai — is liye Extra loss 3 dB rakha hai.

    📶 Frequency planner

    Mimosa ke Spectrum Analyzer mein Combined dabao. Jahan oonchi lines (−80 dBm se oopar) hon, woh ranges yahan likho.

    160 MHz sirf C6x jaise 6 GHz devices mein hota hai — Allowed range device ke hisaab se likho.
